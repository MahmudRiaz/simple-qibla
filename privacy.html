Privacy Policy

Simple Qibla · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: Simple Qibla does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app uses stays on your iPhone.

Location and compass

With your permission, Simple Qibla uses your iPhone’s location and compass while the app is open. It uses them for two things only: working out the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah, and calculating prayer times.

The app keeps your most recent location on your iPhone, in storage that only Simple Qibla and its own widgets can read, so the widgets can show prayer times. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

You can turn off location access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Prayers you mark and your settings

The prayers you tick off, and your chosen calculation method and Asr school, are saved on your iPhone so the app and widgets can show them. They are not sent anywhere.

No accounts, tracking, analytics or ads

The app has no sign-in, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking, and it doesn’t connect to the internet. Prayer times are calculated on the device with the open-source Adhan library.

Backups and deleting your data

Like any app’s data, what Simple Qibla saves may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple controls under its privacy policy. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
