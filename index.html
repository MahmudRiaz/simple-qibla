Simple Qibla

Find the Kaaba & Track Prayers

Simple Qibla points you to the Kaaba in Makkah with one arrow. The screen turns green and your iPhone taps when you’re facing the Qibla. It also shows the time to the next prayer, lets you tick off each prayer, and has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Common questions

The arrow seems off or jumps around
Hold your iPhone flat and away from metal, magnets and cases with magnetic clasps. If the app says the compass needs calibrating, wave your phone in a figure 8 for a few seconds.
Prayer times don’t match my masjid
Open Settings in the app (the gear, bottom-left) and choose the calculation method and Asr school your masjid uses. “Automatic” picks the method most common in your region.
How do I add a widget?
  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search “Qibla”, pick Pray Now or Today’s Prayers, then tap Add Widget.
For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then the widget row under the clock. Widgets need iOS 17 or later.
The widget says to open the app
Open Simple Qibla once and allow location. The widget then uses your last location to work out prayer times.
Does the app need the internet?
No. Direction and prayer times are calculated on your iPhone and work offline.
What happens to my location?
It stays on your iPhone. Simple Qibla has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.
